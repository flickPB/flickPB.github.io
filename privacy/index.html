Flick

Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Flick (also called FlickPB) is an iPhone app for organizing pickleball events: RSVPs, round robins, scores, ratings, and splitting court costs. Flick is operated by Frances Ling, an individual developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Flick collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

The short version: we collect what is needed to run your account and your events. We do not sell your information, we do not show ads, we do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Information we collect

Account and sign-in information

  • Email address, if you sign in with email, Google, or Apple. Used to create your account, sign you in, and send sign-in links. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we receive an Apple relay address instead.
  • Phone number, if you sign in with your phone. Used to text you a one-time sign-in code.
  • Name from Google or Apple, if you sign in that way and the provider shares it. Used as your starting display name.
  • Account identifiers: an internal account ID, a short player code, and the account ID that Google or Apple gives us when you sign in with them. Used to keep your account and your event history together.
  • Password, only if you choose email-and-password sign-in. It is stored by our authentication provider as a one-way hash; we never see it.

Profile information you choose to add

  • Display name, shown to other players in events you join.
  • Optional details: age, city and state, Venmo handle, handedness, an emoji avatar, paddle brand and shape, and a skill self-assessment. Flick does not collect photos; avatars are emoji only.
  • Rating: a skill rating that starts at a default value and is calculated from your match results.

Event activity

  • Events you create: title, location (free text), date and time, format, number of courts, player limit, cost, the Venmo handle to pay, and an optional group name and icon.
  • Participation: whether you joined, were invited, are waitlisted, requested approval, or declined; and the payment status an organizer marks for you (unpaid, requested, or paid).
  • Games: rosters, teams, court assignments, scores, standings, playoff results, and the rating changes that result.
  • Names of other people: organizers can add players who do not have a Flick account by typing their name. If you add someone this way, please only add people who are fine with it.
  • In-app notices, such as being moved off a waitlist or an event being cancelled.

Technical information

  • Our hosting provider (Supabase) keeps server and sign-in logs that may include your IP address, device or browser type, and request times. We use these only to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.
  • The Flick website (GitHub Pages) may log the IP address of visitors who open a shared event link or this page.
  • Your sign-in session is stored on your iPhone in the iOS Keychain so you stay signed in.

What we do not collect

Flick does not access your precise location (GPS), contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or health data. It does not collect bank or card numbers and does not process payments. It does not include analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs. If you have opted in to share diagnostics with Apple, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports under Apple's own terms.

How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To run events: show rosters, manage RSVPs and waitlists, schedule matches, record scores, and calculate standings and ratings.
  • To help players settle court costs by showing Venmo handles and payment status.
  • To keep the service working and safe, including investigating abuse.
  • To answer your questions and requests.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

What other people can see

  • Players in an event can see the event details and its roster, including display names, ratings, scores, and results. Anyone signed in to Flick who has an event's link can open that event.
  • Organizers and players may see your Venmo handle and payment status for events that collect a cost, so payments can be settled.
  • Anyone with an event link, even without the app, can see a public summary on the web: the event title, location, date and time, format, cost, whether RSVP is open or needs approval, and how many spots are filled. The public summary does not show names, ratings, or payment details.

Your email address, phone number, age, city, and other optional profile details are not shown to other players.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run Flick. They process information only to provide their service to us.

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in system, and server functions.
  • Resend delivers sign-in emails on Supabase's behalf.
  • An SMS provider connected to Supabase delivers phone sign-in codes.
  • Google and Apple handle sign-in if you choose "Continue with Google" or Sign in with Apple.
  • Apple MapKit suggests cities as you type a city in your profile; the text you type is sent to Apple to return suggestions.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the Flick website, shared event pages, and this policy.

When you tap a Venmo link, Flick opens the Venmo app or website with the recipient and amount filled in. Any payment happens in Venmo, under Venmo's own privacy policy; Flick does not receive your Venmo account details.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the safety of users or others, or as part of transferring the app to a new operator, who would remain bound by this policy.

Data retention

We keep your account and profile while your account is active. Event and match records are kept so that standings, history, and ratings stay accurate for everyone who played. Server logs are kept for a limited time by our hosting provider and then deleted.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app at Profile > Settings > Delete Account. This permanently deletes your sign-in account (including your email address or phone number) and removes the personal details in your profile. Past match results stay in the events they belong to so other players' standings and ratings remain correct, but they are shown as "Deleted player" and are no longer linked to you.

If you cannot use the app, email hello.francesfling@gmail.com from the address on your account (or tell us the phone number you signed in with) and we will delete your account for you.

Your choices and rights

  • You can view and edit your profile in the app at any time, and leave optional fields blank.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete it by emailing us. We will respond within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, California or the EU), you may have additional rights under local law. We will honor those requests too, and we will not treat you differently for making them.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access rules limit each account to the information it is allowed to see, and sign-in sessions are stored in the iOS Keychain. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected users if a breach requires it.

Children

Flick is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

International users

Flick is operated from the United States. If you use it from elsewhere, your information will be processed in the United States and in the countries where our providers operate.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it here and change the effective date above. If a change significantly affects how we use your information, we will also let you know in the app before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions, data requests, or account deletion requests: Frances Ling, hello.francesfling@gmail.com.

We typically respond within 2 business days.
